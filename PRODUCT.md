# SabiNote — Product Context

## Product Purpose

SabiNote is a curriculum-aligned AI lesson note generator for Nigerian teachers. It ingests the Nigerian state curriculum (NERDC / state-specific scheme of work) and produces structured, print-ready lesson plans and full lesson notes in seconds. The product eliminates the 3-5 hours per week Nigerian teachers spend manually writing lesson notes — a genuine pain point across public and private schools.

## Register

**Mixed.** Landing page: brand. App interior (dashboard, generate, library, wallet, settings): product.

## Users

- **Primary:** Nigerian secondary and primary school teachers (JSS1–SSS3, Pry 1–6), predominantly mobile-first, often on Android.
- **Secondary:** Heads of department reviewing staff notes, school proprietors.
- **Skill level:** Comfortable with WhatsApp and basic apps. Not power users. Functional, clear UIs win.
- **Context:** Logging in during free periods at school, or in the evening at home. Busy, time-pressured, goal-oriented.

## Brand

- **Name:** SabiNote (Pidgin: "to know how to write notes")
- **By:** Parakletus Technologies
- **Tone:** Confident, warm, professional. Not startup-cute. Not corporate-cold. Like a brilliant colleague who gets things done.
- **Anti-tone:** No hype language. No "elevate", "seamless", "revolutionize". Plain verbs.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed over decoration.** Every screen has one job. No distractions.
2. **Trust through precision.** Curriculum labels, term/week numbers, class levels — exact and correct.
3. **Warmth without informality.** This is a professional tool. Emojis in body text undermine credibility.
4. **The phone is the computer.** Design mobile-first. Desktop is secondary.

## Color Strategy

**Committed.** Brand purple (`#641BC4` / `oklch(40% 0.22 290)`) carries 30–50% of the product surface. One accent. No competing colors. The purple is the identity.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS cream/beige with pastel accents
- "AI purple gradient" everything — purple gradients everywhere signals generic AI product
- Identical card grids with icon + heading + text
- Hero-metric dashboards (big number, small label, gradient card)
- Startup emoji in body copy
- Modal-first interactions

## Strategic Principles

- The generation flow (Configure → Plan → Note) is the product's core loop. Make it fast and rewarding.
- Wallet friction is real — if the balance display is unclear, teachers abandon. Show balance prominently and honestly.
- The note canvas is where value is delivered. It must feel like a professional document editor, not a chat bubble.
